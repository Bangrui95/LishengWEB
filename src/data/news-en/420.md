All employees of Lisheng Group wish everyone a happy New Year!

As the sun and moon open a new era and heaven and earth greet a new day, all of us at Lisheng Group sincerely thank our customers, old and new, for their support, and wish you a 2017 of fulfilled wishes, smooth work and family happiness. Over the past year we weathered ups and downs together; it is precisely because of your support and trust that we have achieved today's fruits.

In the new year of 2017, we will work harder and strive further, firmly believing that our efforts will create a different sky. May Lisheng continue this beautiful bond with you in 2017. Finally, once again we wish you and your family good health, fulfilled wishes and all the best in the new year.
