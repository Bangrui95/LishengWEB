On 29 October 2016, Grain & Oil Market News released the sixth China Grain & Oil List, on which Shandong Lisheng Food Group Co., Ltd. was named a "2016 China Top 10 Grain & Oil (Food) Brand."

The "China Top 10 Grain & Oil (Food) Brand" is an influential national, third-party award in the flour and noodle industry, offering overseas importers a trustworthy quality endorsement. For over two decades Lisheng has upheld its philosophy of "select fine grain, make good Lisheng noodles," controlling quality through its quality-management system and 5S processes and continuously introducing internationally advanced milling and inspection equipment to safeguard consistency from raw grain to finished product. Together with ISO 9001, ISO 22000 and HACCP certification, Lisheng is a reliable manufacturing partner for noodle and flour export and OEM / private-label cooperation.

**About Lisheng**

Founded in 1995, Shandong Lisheng Group is an integrated group enterprise spanning grain processing, deep processing, storage, logistics and sales. Today it operates 8 subsidiaries, processes 7,000 tons of wheat and produces 560 tons of dried noodles per day, with annual revenue of around RMB 3 billion. The group is certified to ISO 9001, ISO 22000 and HACCP, with full traceability from raw grain to finished product, and supplies overseas importers, supermarkets and foodservice channels with stable, compliant bulk products as well as OEM / private-label contract manufacturing customised in formulation, specification and packaging.
