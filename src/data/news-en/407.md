On 25 August 2013, the packaging system of the flour workshop and the equipment of the new noodle workshop at the Jining plant started up and passed their trial run, marking the largest and most highly automated technical upgrade in the group's history.

The upgrade introduced international automation control systems from Schneider, Siemens and ABB, and brought online 850- and 650-type premium dried-noodle lines together with two new lines for noodle sheets and non-fried instant noodles; the flour workshop achieved centralised production, centralised storage and computerised automatic dispatch, raising the overall process equipment to a domestic-leading level. High automation and low human intervention deliver batch-to-batch consistency — the core capability for handling export and OEM contract-manufacturing orders. A diversified product mix (premium dried noodles, noodle sheets, non-fried instant noodles) also lets Lisheng serve the private-label needs of different overseas channels including supermarkets, foodservice and convenience foods, with customisation from formulation and specification to packaging.

**About Lisheng**

Founded in 1995, Shandong Lisheng Group is an integrated group enterprise spanning grain processing, deep processing, storage, logistics and sales. Today it operates 8 subsidiaries, processes 7,000 tons of wheat and produces 560 tons of dried noodles per day, with annual revenue of around RMB 3 billion. The group is certified to ISO 9001, ISO 22000 and HACCP, with full traceability from raw grain to finished product, and supplies overseas importers, supermarkets and foodservice channels with stable, compliant bulk products as well as OEM / private-label contract manufacturing customised in formulation, specification and packaging.
