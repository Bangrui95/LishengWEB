As the Year of the Snake approaches, on behalf of all employees of Lisheng Group I extend New Year greetings to the leaders at every level and friends from all walks of life who care for and support Lisheng's development. May you enjoy good fortune, happiness and health in the new year.

In 2013, drawing on top-class domestic equipment, advanced production technology and a strong R&D, quality-control and production team, Lisheng controlled every stage of production strictly to food-safety requirements and achieved considerable progress. We launched a series of products that won wide recognition and high praise from the market, distributors and consumers. Amid fierce competition, total sales in 2012 grew 20% over 2011. Building on that foundation, in 2013 Lisheng intensified product R&D, extended its product chain, and worked to keep quality stable and meet market demand — ensuring product quality and food safety, reassuring the government and satisfying customers and consumers.

For the achievements of today, we first thank the leaders at every level and government departments for their strong support and care, and even more the distributors and consumers across the country for their concern and goodwill. In 2013, Lisheng people will work together with friends from all sectors to make China's food industry stronger and larger, and to stand at the forefront of the world food industry.

Once again, our sincere wishes to leaders and friends from all walks of life for a happy New Year and a joyful family.
