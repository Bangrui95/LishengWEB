On the morning of 11 July 2022, Han Shuhuan, an elderly villager from Nanyangzhuang, was clipped by a vehicle while on the road and fell, pinned under his electric tricycle and unable to get up. Two of our employees happened to see it, immediately stopped to help, lifted the vehicle and the man clear, and — after confirming he was unhurt — returned to their work.

After a month of recovery, Han Shuhuan remained grateful to the helpful Lisheng people, and on 22 August personally brought a banner and a letter of thanks to the company — which is how we learned of the good deed by employees Wang Jianshun and Wang Mingyu. Their actions embodied the fine Lisheng qualities of "dedication and responsibility" and put into practice the corporate mission of "doing work that benefits people's lives." While commending and rewarding them, Lisheng Group also called on all employees to carry forward the corporate spirit of "do good and think of others," bringing more warmth and help to society.
