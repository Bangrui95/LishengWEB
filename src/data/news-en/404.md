On 11 March 2014, with a gentle spring breeze and budding plants, more than 50 employees of our group took part in a 12 March tree-planting activity organised by the group's enterprise-management centre. Flags fluttered, and the employees in Lisheng red became the brightest scene along the Yangjia River.

At the planting site everyone worked together with great enthusiasm, each sapling carrying green hope. The activity once again exercised the team spirit of unity and, more importantly, showcased Lisheng's vigorous, upward enterprise image.
