On 20 January 2012, Shandong Lisheng Group's 2012 Work-Goals and 2011 Annual Commendation Conference was grandly held in the Yumancheng banquet hall. More than a hundred employees — leaders and outstanding workers from the companies, centres and departments — gathered to share the occasion.

General Manager Mr Gao Fengshe presented a report on "The Group's Development Plan and 2012 Goals" and read out the "Decision to Commend Advanced Units and Individuals of 2011." Company leaders, outstanding department heads and representatives of advanced individuals then gave excellent remarks. Chairman Mr Gao Fengguo signed 2012 target-responsibility agreements with the Jining, Chengwu and Wenshang companies and the marketing centre, and delivered an address to the conference.
