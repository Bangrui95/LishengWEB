Shandong Lisheng Group's expansion project for 300 tons/day of premium dried noodles, noodle sheets and non-fried instant noodles has passed its acceptance review and entered full operation.

Beyond premium dried noodles, the expansion adds noodle-sheet and non-fried instant-noodle capacity, broadening the product mix available to export and contract-manufacturing customers: dried noodles suit supermarket and retail channels, while noodle sheets and non-fried instant noodles serve convenience-food and foodservice channels — meeting overseas customers' needs for multi-category, one-stop sourcing and private-label supply.

**About Lisheng**

Founded in 1995, Shandong Lisheng Group is an integrated group enterprise spanning grain processing, deep processing, storage, logistics and sales. Today it operates 8 subsidiaries, processes 7,000 tons of wheat and produces 560 tons of dried noodles per day, with annual revenue of around RMB 3 billion. The group is certified to ISO 9001, ISO 22000 and HACCP, with full traceability from raw grain to finished product, and supplies overseas importers, supermarkets and foodservice channels with stable, compliant bulk products as well as OEM / private-label contract manufacturing customised in formulation, specification and packaging.
