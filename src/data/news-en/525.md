Shandong Lisheng Food Group Co., Ltd.'s expansion project for 300 tons per day of premium dried noodles, noodle sheets and non-fried instant noodles has successfully passed acceptance.

Beyond premium dried noodles, this 300-ton/day expansion added noodle-sheet and non-fried instant-noodle capacity, further enriching the product mix available to export and OEM customers — dried noodles suit supermarket and retail channels, while noodle sheets and non-fried instant noodles serve instant-food and foodservice channels, meeting overseas clients' needs for multi-category, one-stop sourcing and private-label production. The group now comprises eight companies, processing 7,000 tons of wheat and producing 560 tons of dried noodles daily, all managed under ISO 9001, ISO 22000 and HACCP systems with full traceability from raw grain to finished product — providing overseas importers, supermarkets and foodservice channels with stable, compliant volume supply and customisation of formulation, specifications and packaging.
