On the afternoon of 27 March, Jining Deputy Mayor Zhou Hong, accompanied by She Chunming — Party Working Committee Secretary and Management Committee Director of the High-Tech Zone — visited Lisheng Group headquarters to inspect and study how High-Tech Zone enterprises are running e-commerce.

Deputy Mayor Zhou heard a report from General Manager Gao Fengshe, who introduced the structure of Lisheng's e-commerce department, the platforms it had joined, and the results of its first three months, expressing confidence that with the government's strong attention and the support of relevant departments, Lisheng's e-commerce would grow and thrive.

Yao Weixing, director of the group's supermarket department, gave the deputy mayor a detailed introduction to the categories and features of Lisheng's flour and noodles. Mayor Zhou suggested designing more packaging formats suited to online shopping and logistics. Sales Director Zang Ming reported on the workflow and development plan of the e-commerce department. Mayor Zhou noted that e-commerce is the trend — online procurement and transactions are growing ever larger — and urged the company to seize the opportunity and develop it vigorously. High-Tech Zone Deputy Director Li Zheng and Economic Development Bureau Director Zhang Mo joined the inspection.
