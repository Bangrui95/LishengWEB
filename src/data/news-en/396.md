On the morning of the 28th, carrying concern for fellow citizens in the disaster area and confidence in rebuilding a new home in Yushu, the headquarters of Shandong Lisheng Group — at the initiative and under the leadership of Chairman Gao Fengguo and General Manager Gao Fengshe — saw staff donate enthusiastically to the Yushu earthquake-stricken area of Qinghai, expressing their love and supporting reconstruction with concrete action to help the people through their hardship.

At the donation ceremony, Lisheng employees voiced their wishes, pledging to stand with the people of Yushu "through thick and thin," hoping the disaster-area residents would rebuild their beautiful homes soon, and helping to kindle hope and firm confidence amid the disaster.
