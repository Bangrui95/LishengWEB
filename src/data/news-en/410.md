On 8 June 2013, Shandong Lisheng Group's mid-2013 management performance-review meeting was held in the third-floor conference room, attended by Chairman Gao Fengguo and the managers of the companies and centres.

The managers reviewed the first half's human-resources and management situation and earnestly summarised the urgent problems facing the group and their own management shortcomings. Afterwards, Chairman Gao Fengguo affirmed their achievements, pointed out their problems, and instructed all managers to follow the set work plans strictly, striving for greater progress in the second half and working to meet the year's goals.
