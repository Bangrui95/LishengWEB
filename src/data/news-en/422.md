As the motherland's 67th anniversary approaches in 2016, all employees of Lisheng Group wish the customers and friends who care for and support the group a happy National Day, family happiness, good health and all the best.

Looking back on our extraordinary journey over the years, we owe special thanks to you. Our flour and noodle products have won the trust of consumers; twenty years of development and accumulation have built our brand's reputation. As we celebrate National Day, we especially thank you for your sincere cooperation and your steadfast trust and support.

Looking ahead, we shoulder the task of expanding our business to higher, broader and deeper levels. We will always keep our promise of integrity to customers and make our product quality even better. Finally, our most sincere wishes to everyone for a happy National Day holiday, family well-being, thriving business and ever-greater success.
