As the auspicious Snake sees out the old year and the golden Horse ushers in spring, we step into a hopeful 2014. On this New Year occasion, on behalf of all employees of Lisheng Group, I extend greetings to the leaders at every level and friends from all walks of life who care for and support Lisheng. May you enjoy great fortune in the Year of the Horse.

2013 was a year of leapfrog progress for Lisheng. The group invested over 30 million yuan in product-technology upgrades and workshop equipment renovation, put large new plants into use, introduced modern equipment, further raised production capacity, and steadily improved product quality. Drawing on an efficient R&D, quality-control and production team, it developed several new products to meet market demand — well received by distributors and consumers — and, amid fierce competition, smoothly met its annual sales targets, keeping the group on a fast, healthy and steady path.

Looking back on 2013, it is precisely because of your trust and support that Lisheng achieved such fruits. In the new year, Lisheng people will, with a vigorous spirit and a more pragmatic work style, pursue leapfrog development, create more value for society, and provide consumers with more satisfying, reassuring green food.

Looking ahead to 2014, let us advance hand in hand, seek development together, and create brilliance in unity. Once again, sincere wishes to leaders and friends from all sectors for a happy New Year and a joyful family.
