Liangshan Lisheng Food Co., Ltd., a subsidiary of Shandong Lisheng Group, processes 3,000 tons of wheat per day using a complete Bühler intelligent flour production system with full flour-blending technology.

Liangshan Lisheng uses a complete Bühler intelligent flour production system and full flour-blending process — Bühler being an internationally recognised premium milling-equipment brand, highly regarded by overseas flour and wheat-food buyers. Full flour-blending means consistent flour quality and the flexibility to formulate dedicated flours — bread flour, noodle-specific flour, pastry flour and more — to a client's requirements, making it easy to take on export orders and OEM new-product development. Combined with the group's ISO 22000 and HACCP food-safety systems and multi-base premium wheat sources, Lisheng can offer overseas brand clients one-stop flour contract manufacturing — from formula design and dedicated-flour customisation to private-label packaging.
