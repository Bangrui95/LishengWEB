To welcome International Workers' Day and celebrate the May Day holiday, Lisheng Group organised its companies in a group-wide clean-up, thoroughly tidying and cleaning the plants, office buildings and workshops.

Through this activity, all Lisheng employees stretched their striving muscles and showed the vitality of "labour is most glorious." Lisheng people gained not only an elegant, tidy working environment but also a fuller, more enthusiastic working state for the days ahead. With such a positive, upward team, Lisheng Group will only grow more brilliant.
