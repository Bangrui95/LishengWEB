On 21 February, the sixth day of the first lunar month and the first workday after the Spring Festival holiday, Lisheng Group and its subsidiaries held "refocus" meetings via morning assemblies and pre-shift gatherings, mobilising staff to quickly adjust their mindset and throw themselves into intense, orderly production. Employees at all levels swiftly returned from the holiday mood to their work — "on the job every minute, every second on duty" — while managers went to the front line to supervise. Grassroots units likewise held refocus meetings through team-leader and pre-shift gatherings.

In line with the group's requirements, the subsidiaries and departments held refocus meetings, again setting out 2018 goals, unifying thinking, and starting work immediately — turning work into target tasks, tasks into implementation, and implementation into real assessment — to ensure a good start to every task and to pursue all work in concerted effort.
